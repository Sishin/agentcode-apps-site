Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Learn Malayalam (“the App”) handles user information.

1. Overview

Learn Malayalam is an educational application designed to help users learn Malayalam alphabets and basic concepts through interactive activities and games.

The App is suitable for users aged 3 years and above.

2. Information Collection and Use

The App does not collect, store, use, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal information (such as name, email address, phone number)
  • Location information
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics data
  • Cookies or tracking data

3. Internet Connectivity

The App functions entirely offline. It does not require an internet connection and does not transmit any data to external servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services, including:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics tools
  • Social media integrations
  • External SDKs that collect user data

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is designed for a general audience, including children aged 3 and above. Since the App does not collect personal information from any users, it complies with applicable child privacy regulations including the Google Play Families Policy.

6. Data Security

As the App does not collect or transmit any personal data, no user data is stored or processed.
