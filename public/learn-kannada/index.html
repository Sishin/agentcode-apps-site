Features

Everything your child needs to learn the alphabet

Learn Kannada is made for parents teaching Kannada at home, one small step at a time.

Trace letters the right way

Every letter has a guided tracing road that follows the usual Kannada writing order: the body first, the talakattu (head mark) last.

  • Guided mode: road, start dot, arrows and a guide finger
  • Dots mode: follow the dotted line on your own
  • Free mode: write the letter alone, then tap Check
  • Drawing board with crayon, marker, rainbow and glitter pens

A learning path from easy to tricky

Letters are grouped into 12 short lessons that start with round, simple shapes like ಠ ರ ಬ ಲ and build up to tricky ones.

  • Up to three stars for each letter
  • A “needs a little practice” list brings back letters worth another try
  • All 13 vowels, ಅಂ and ಅಃ, and 34 consonants, each with sound, picture and example word

Six playful learning games

  • First Letter: pick the letter a picture's word starts with
  • Sound Quiz: listen and tap the letter you hear
  • Memory Match: match each letter with its picture
  • Odd One Out: spot the look-alike, like ಪ and ಫ
  • Letter Train: put letters in alphabet order
  • Balloon Pop: pop the balloons with the right letter

Stories to read and listen to

Five short illustrated stories in simple Kannada, each page with an English line for parents. “Read to me” reads every page aloud.

  • The Thirsty Crow (ಬಾಯಾರಿದ ಕಾಗೆ)
  • The Ant and the Dove
  • The Hare and the Tortoise
  • The Lion and the Mouse
  • The Two Goats

A little practice every day

A daily goal, a day streak and stars keep children motivated, and the home screen always shows the next letter to learn. Letter and word sounds use your device's Kannada voice.
